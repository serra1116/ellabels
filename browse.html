Definitions

In this agreement, "Renter" refers to the individual who has booked the vehicle through MobiLeasePH; "Owner" refers to the registered vehicle owner listed on this contract; "Vehicle" refers to the specific car described in the booking details; "Platform" refers to MobiLeasePH and its digital services.

Booking Confirmation
  • This contract is binding upon the Renter's digital signature and the Owner's confirmation.
  • The Owner reserves the right to cancel the booking within 24 hours of submission with a full refund of the down payment.
  • The Renter will receive an official booking confirmation via email once the Owner approves the reservation.
  • MobiLeasePH acts solely as the booking platform and is not party to the vehicle rental agreement.
Payment Terms
  • Down Payment: A non-refundable 30% down payment is required to secure the booking. This payment has been acknowledged by the Renter as part of the booking process.
  • Remaining Balance: The remaining 70% of the rental fee is due on the pick-up date via the method selected by the Renter.
  • Security Deposit: A refundable deposit of PHP 5,000 is collected on pick-up day. This will be returned within 3-5 business days after the vehicle passes inspection upon return.
  • All payments must be made in Philippine Peso (PHP). Foreign currency is not accepted.
  • Deductions from the security deposit may apply for damages, excessive cleaning, late returns, or policy violations.
Cancellation & Refund Policy
  • Free Cancellation: Cancel at least 24 hours before the pick-up time to receive a full refund of the remaining balance. The down payment is non-refundable.
  • Late Cancellation: Cancellations made less than 24 hours before the start of the rental are non-refundable for the full booking amount.
  • No-Show: If the Renter fails to pick up the vehicle without prior notice, the booking is forfeited and no refund will be issued.
  • Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days to the original payment method.
Vehicle Use & Renter Obligations
  • The Renter must be at least 21 years of age and hold a valid Philippine LTO driver's license for self-drive bookings.
  • The Vehicle must be returned at the agreed drop-off location and time in the same condition as received, with the same fuel level.
  • The Renter is fully responsible for all traffic violations, toll fees, and parking fees incurred during the rental period.
  • The Vehicle must not be driven outside of the agreed territory without prior written consent from the Owner.
  • In the event of an accident or breakdown, the Renter must immediately notify the Owner and relevant authorities.
  • Smoking inside the vehicle is strictly prohibited at all times.
  • Pets and animals are not permitted inside the vehicle.
  • Sub-renting or lending the vehicle to a third party is prohibited.
  • The Vehicle must not be used for illegal activities, racing, or off-road driving unless specifically permitted by the Owner.
Late Return Policy
  • A late return fee of PHP 200 per hour applies for returns beyond the agreed drop-off time.
  • If the vehicle is returned more than 4 hours late, it will be treated as an additional day's rental and charged accordingly at the full daily rate.
  • The Renter must notify the Owner as early as possible if a late return is anticipated.
Damage & Liability
  • The Renter is fully liable for any damage to the Vehicle caused during the rental period, including damage caused by third parties while the Vehicle is in the Renter's possession.
  • A vehicle inspection will be conducted at both pick-up and drop-off. Any discrepancies will be documented and charges applied accordingly.
  • The basic insurance included in the rental covers third-party liability only. Damage to the Owner's vehicle is the Renter's responsibility.
  • In the event of theft or total loss of the Vehicle, the Renter bears full financial responsibility.
Fuel Policy

The Vehicle is provided with a full tank of fuel. It must be returned with the same fuel level. Failure to do so will result in a fuel top-up charge plus a PHP 150 refueling service fee deducted from the security deposit.

Privacy & Data

Personal information collected during the booking process, including identification documents, license details, and payment information, is handled in accordance with the MobiLeasePH Privacy Policy and the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173). Data is used solely for the purpose of completing the rental transaction and will not be shared with third parties without the Renter's consent.

Governing Law

This agreement shall be governed by the laws of the Republic of the Philippines. Any disputes arising from this contract shall be settled through mutual negotiation or, failing that, through the appropriate courts in the Philippines.
